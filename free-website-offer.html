Questions People

Is the website really free?

Yes. The build itself is free — design, copy, and launch. The $2,000 setup fee is waived for the businesses in this launch group.

The only thing you pay is $97/month, and that starts once your site is live. Nothing up front, nothing due before you see the work.

What exactly does the $97/month cover?

Four things: hosting, ongoing maintenance and edits, support from a real person here in Lake Charles, and MultiplyOS — the platform that runs your missed-call text-back, follow-up automations, pipeline, chat widget, and mobile app.

It's one flat price. No per-user fees, no upsells to unlock the features above.

What is MultiplyOS?

It's the system that sits behind your website and does the follow-up you don't have time for — texting back missed calls in seconds, chasing leads that went quiet, and keeping every conversation in one inbox you can run from your phone.

A website brings people to the door. MultiplyOS is what keeps them from wandering off to your competitor.

How long until my site is live?

Days, not months. Once we have your details — services, service area, photos if you have them — we handle the writing and the design and get a draft in front of you quickly.

The main thing that slows a build down is waiting on information from you, so the faster you get us what we need, the faster you're live.

What do you need from me?

Not much, and not much of your time. A short conversation about what you do and who you serve, any photos of your work, and your logo if you have one. If you don't have photos or a logo, we'll work with what you've got.

You don't need to write anything. That's our job.

I already have a website. Can you still help?

Usually yes — and this is who we help most often. Most of the businesses we work with already have a site; it just isn't bringing the phone to life.

We'll build you a new one and move everything over. Bring it up on the call and we'll tell you straight whether replacing it is actually worth it for you.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. There's no contract and no minimum term. You can cancel anytime with 30 days notice.

That's deliberate — if the site isn't earning its keep, we'd rather you be free to walk than stuck. It keeps the pressure on us to make it work.

Do I own the website?

Yes — stay with us for 24 months and the website is yours to keep.

If you decide to leave before then, you can buy it outright and take it with you. Either way you're not stuck and you don't walk away empty-handed. We'll walk you through exactly how that works on the call.

Do you work with my type of business?

We work with any service-based business. Home services are our bread and butter — plumbing, HVAC, power washing, lawn care, roofing, electrical — and we've built for attorneys and plenty of other local service businesses too.

If you make your money from the phone ringing and jobs getting booked, we're a fit.

The one exception is e-commerce. We don't build online stores — everything we do is built around booking jobs and following up with customers, not carts and inventory.

Why only 10 businesses?

Because we actually build these ourselves, and we won't take on more than we can do well. Ten at a time is what we can give real attention to.
